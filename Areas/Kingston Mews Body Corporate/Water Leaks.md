# Water Leaks

## Situation
- **Where:** south section, on a hillside next to a nature reserve.
- **History:** several months of visible bursts plus undetectable underground failures.
- **Assessment:** likely systemic and driven by workmanship.

## Recommended diagnostics
| Test | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Bulk meter vs submeter reconciliation | How much water is lost between the scheme meter and the unit meters |
| Minimum-night-flow test | Flow when usage should be near zero, which means leakage |
